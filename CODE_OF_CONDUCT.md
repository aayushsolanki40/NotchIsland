# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participating in NotchIsland to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming, and assume good intent.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **aayushsolanki40@gmail.com**. All reports are
reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or
contributions, or ban contributors, who don't follow this code.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including its enforcement guidelines, is available at
the link above.
